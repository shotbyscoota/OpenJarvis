# Research notes (source of truth for numbers in the playbooks)

Gathered 2026-10-27 from web search. Most sources are third-party blogs, not
the platforms themselves. Verify against official pages before relying on any
line marked *unverified*.

## CAN-SPAM (general knowledge, not from a search result)
- Applies to B2B email as well as consumer email; no prior consent needed in the US.
- Requires: accurate From/routing info, non-deceptive subject, identified as an ad,
  valid physical postal address, working opt-out.
- Opt-outs honored within **10 business days**.
- Some states add their own rules. Not legal advice.

## Google Places data
- Places API returns at most **60 results per query** (vendor claim).
- Vendor claims Google terms bar bulk scraping and limit caching to **30 days**
  for most fields. *Unverified*: read Google's live Maps Platform terms.

## Etsy (digital downloads)
- AI-made digital products are allowed but need disclosure (vendor guides):
  set "Designed by seller" and add an AI note to the description.
- Reported Oct 2026 tightening: bot-generated listings banned; digital downloads
  need a preview/excerpt. *Unverified*: source is a competitor vendor blog.
- No source names coloring pages in a dedicated rule.

## Amazon KDP (coloring books)
- AI-generated images/text/translations must be disclosed at title setup.
  Disclosure is not shown publicly (third-party claim).
- Common rejection causes: broken line work, gray shading, near-duplicate pages,
  interior under **24 pages**, file not **8.5" x 11"**.
- Claim of a cap of **3 titles per day** before flagging: *unverified*, single source.
- No source found on current category saturation.
