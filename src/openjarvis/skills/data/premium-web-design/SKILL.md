# Premium web design

## Direction first
Before any code, write a 5-line brief: audience, the single action the visitor should take, three brand adjectives, a type pairing, and a palette of at most 5 colors with contrast checked (WCAG AA). If the owner gave no brand, derive it from their trade and city, not from a generic default.

## Craft rules
- Real hierarchy: one dominant element per screen, generous whitespace, a consistent spacing scale.
- Two typefaces at most; fluid type scale; line length 45-75 characters.
- Layouts must be mobile-first and work with no JavaScript for core content.
- Imagery: use the owner's real photos when supplied. Otherwise use clearly marked placeholders and tell the owner; do not pass stock or generated images off as their work.
- Motion is subtle and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Avoid: centered hero over gradient with three icon cards, lorem ipsum, fake testimonials, fake awards.

## Conversion copy
- Headline states the outcome for the customer, in their words.
- Every section answers a question: what, why trust, how, what next.
- One primary call to action repeated, with a phone and a form path.
- Only claims the owner can back up.

## Self-review
Render, screenshot and critique with the `site-screenshot-review` skill before reporting done. Be honest about what is below premium and what the owner must supply (photos, reviews, logo).
